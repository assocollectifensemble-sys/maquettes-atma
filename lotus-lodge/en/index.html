The lodge

A tropical lodge on stilts, open to the sea

Lotus Lodge Ocean View is a tropical-style lodge on stilts, tucked into a tropical garden with a sea view. Across 50 m², enjoy a private entrance, a lounge area, a private kitchen, a patio, a terrace and a balcony, with views of the sea, the garden, the mountains and the courtyard.

Raw wood, natural linen, palms and a sheer canopy net: every detail invites you to slow down. Shops, a bus stop and village life are all close by. It is the ideal Rodrigues accommodation with a sea view for couples, friends and families alike.

Your host, Jean-Noël, a lover of nature and good food, welcomes you in English and French and happily shares favourite local spots.

Why Rodrigues

Rodrigues, the unspoilt island of the Republic of Mauritius

A small volcanic island in the Indian Ocean, Rodrigues keeps a peaceful, authentic way of life. A Rodrigues holiday means nature on your doorstep and the warm welcome of the Rodriguans.

A vast lagoon

A huge turquoise lagoon surrounds the island: swimming, snorkelling and boat trips to the islets.

The islets

Île aux Cocos, Île aux Sables and more: protected islets to explore on excursions, home to seabirds.

Hiking

Coastal trails, green hills and hidden coves: the island is made for walking, with sweeping lagoon views.

Diving

Dive sites to explore with local dive centres, among coral and tropical fish.

Kitesurfing

Steady trade winds make Rodrigues a renowned kitesurf spot, especially in the south-east of the island.

Grand Baie and Port Mathurin

Grand Baie beach is 700 m from the lodge, and the small capital Port Mathurin, 3.5 km away, offers shops and a lively market.

Getting here

Reaching Rodrigues from Mauritius, Réunion and beyond

From Réunion Island

Air Austral links Réunion and Rodrigues with direct flights, seasonal or regular depending on the period. Check schedules and dates on the airline's website, then follow our 3 steps below.

From Mauritius

Air Mauritius connects Mauritius and Rodrigues with regular flights. Check schedules on the airline's website to pick the best time for you.

From Europe and further afield

Fly first to Mauritius or Réunion, then take a connecting flight to Rodrigues. Sir Gaëtan Duval Airport is 19 km from the lodge: send us your flight time and we will suggest the easiest way to reach us.

Where to find us

Jeantac, near Port Mathurin, Rodrigues Island (Republic of Mauritius). Grand Baie beach 700 m · Port Mathurin market 3.5 km · Sir Gaëtan Duval Airport 19 km · shops, bus stop and village life close by.

FAQ

Your questions, our answers

How many guests can stay?

Up to 3 guests: the lodge has a double bed and a sofa bed. A family of 2 adults, 1 child and 1 baby also fits comfortably.

Do you welcome babies?

Yes! A baby cot is provided free of charge on request, subject to availability. Simply mention it in your booking message.

Are children welcome?

Children of all ages are welcome. Let us know their ages when you book and we will prepare the right bedding for your family.

Can I arrive late in the evening?

Yes, check-in is available 24 hours a day. Let us know your arrival time in advance, for example your flight time, and Jean-Noël will arrange your welcome. Check-out is between 10 am and 12 pm.

How close is the beach?

Grand Baie beach is 700 m from the lodge, within walking distance. The turquoise lagoon surrounds the whole island.

How do I get around the island?

A bus stop is close to the lodge, along with shops and village life. To explore Rodrigues at your own pace, rent a car or a scooter: free private parking awaits you on site. Port Mathurin market is 3.5 km away.

How do I get here from the airport?

Sir Gaëtan Duval Airport is 19 km away. Send us your flight time and we will suggest the easiest way to reach the lodge.

How much is a night?

From about €73 per night (benchmark taken from Booking in November 2026, for 2 adults). The exact rate depends on your dates: ask us for a quote in 1 message on WhatsApp.

Can I cook during my stay?

Yes, the private kitchen is equipped with a fridge, microwave, kettle, toaster and utensils: perfect for enjoying fresh island produce on the terrace, facing the sea.

Are pets welcome?

Yes, pets are welcome on prior request. Message us before booking and we will confirm the details of your stay together.

Is there parking?

Yes, free private parking is available on site, handy if you rent a car or scooter to explore Rodrigues.

Is WiFi included?

Yes, WiFi is free throughout your stay.

Is smoking allowed?

The home is entirely smoke-free, keeping the interior fresh and pleasant for every guest.

How do I book?

Two ways: book direct on WhatsApp at +230 5807 1518 and get a personal reply from Jean-Noël, or book online on Booking.
