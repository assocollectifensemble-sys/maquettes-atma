Things to do

Things to do in Rodrigues: our favourite ways to enjoy the island

A vast lagoon, protected islets, limestone caves, coastal trails and a colourful market: Rodrigues is an island to enjoy outdoors. Here are the highlights we recommend to our guests, from our lodge in Jeantac.

Île aux Cocos, a seabird sanctuary

Off the west coast, Île aux Cocos is a protected islet and a haven for seabirds. You visit it on a guided boat trip across the lagoon, with white sand, turquoise water and birdwatching. Access is through an authorised operator, so book your trip in advance; Jean-Noël is happy to point you in the right direction.

Île Gombrani and the southern islets

In the southern lagoon, Île Gombrani is the largest of the southern islets. Boat and pirogue trips combine swimming and snorkelling in crystal-clear water. Ask locally which trips are running during your stay.

François Leguat Reserve and its giant tortoises

In the south-west of the island, the François Leguat Reserve brings Rodrigues' original nature back to life: giant tortoises roaming freely, replanted native vegetation and the Grande Caverne, a limestone cave explored on a guided tour. A wonderful family outing; check visiting hours before you go.

Caverne Patate

Another famous underground site, Caverne Patate is visited with a guide at set times during the day. Stalactites, stalagmites and galleries tell the geological story of the island. Wear sturdy shoes and confirm tour times locally.

Port Mathurin market on Saturday morning

On Saturday mornings from first light, Port Mathurin market is the island's big get-together: fruit, vegetables, chillies, honey and local produce. Arrive early for the best choice. The market is 3.5 km from the lodge, and with the private kitchen you can cook your finds on the terrace.

Hiking and the coastal trail to Trou d'Argent

Rodrigues is an island for walkers. The east coast trail between Graviers and Saint-François follows coves and cliffs to Trou d'Argent, a small cove reached on foot. Inland, the hills offer views over the whole lagoon. Set off early with water and a hat.

Diving, snorkelling and the lagoon

The lagoon surrounding the island is a huge playground: snorkelling near the beaches, boat trips and diving with local dive centres among coral and tropical fish. Closest to the lodge, Grand Baie beach is just 700 m away.

Kitesurfing at Anse Mourouk

In the south-east, Anse Mourouk is the reference kitesurf spot of Rodrigues, with a wide lagoon and steady trade winds, especially during the southern winter, roughly May to October. Local schools welcome beginners and experienced riders alike. Mourouk is an easy drive or scooter ride from Jeantac.

How many days to explore Rodrigues?

Allow 4 days for the highlights and a week to see everything at your own pace. Coming from Mauritius? Our Rodrigues travel guide from Mauritius covers flights, budget and the best season.

FAQ

Your questions, our answers

How do I visit Île aux Cocos?

On a guided boat trip with an authorised operator, as the islet is a protected reserve. Book ahead during holiday periods.

When is Port Mathurin market?

The main market takes place on Saturday mornings from first light. Arrive early for the best choice of fresh produce.

Where can I kitesurf in Rodrigues?

At Anse Mourouk in the south-east, with a wide lagoon and steady trade winds, especially from May to October. Local schools offer lessons.

What can families do in Rodrigues?

The giant tortoises of the François Leguat Reserve, Grand Baie beach, snorkelling in the lagoon and Port Mathurin market are all big hits with children.

How many days do I need in Rodrigues?

4 days for the highlights, a week to explore the island at a relaxed pace, including islets and hikes.
