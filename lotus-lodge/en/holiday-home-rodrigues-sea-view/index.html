The lodge

A sea view holiday home in Rodrigues, on stilts in a tropical garden

Lotus Lodge Ocean View is 50 m² of timber, linen and light, raised on stilts in the heart of the Domaine des Bougainvilliers in Jeantac. A private, self-catering home for 1 to 3 guests, with the sea on the horizon.

The lodge at a glance

  • 50 m² with a private entrance, in a quiet tropical garden
  • 1 bedroom: double bed + sofa bed, for up to 3 guests (or 2 adults, 1 child and 1 baby)
  • Free baby cot on request, subject to availability
  • 1 bathroom with a glass shower
  • Private equipped kitchen, lounge and dining area
  • Terrace, balcony and patio, with views of the sea, garden, mountains and courtyard
  • Free WiFi and free private parking on site
  • 24-hour check-in, check-out between 10 am and 12 pm

The bedroom: a canopy bed and morning light

The bedroom opens onto the terrace through a wide glass door. The double bed, dressed in white linen embroidered with palms, sleeps beneath a sheer mosquito net, and its raw timber frame gives it the feel of a four-poster. Windows look onto the garden greenery, a ceiling fan stirs the soft island air, and two wall lamps create a gentle glow in the evening.

For a third guest, the sofa bed unfolds in the lounge area. Travelling with a baby? A cot is provided free of charge on request.

The lounge and private kitchen

A timber and rattan daybed, palm-print cushions and a round dining table make the lounge a lovely spot for reading and long conversations. The private kitchen has a hob, sink, fridge, microwave, kettle, toaster and utensils: everything you need for breakfast and for cooking the fruit, vegetables and fish from Port Mathurin market, 3.5 km away, before dinner on the terrace.

The bathroom

You step in through tied-back linen curtains. A glass rain shower, a vessel basin on a timber counter and driftwood decor make a bright bathroom in the same tropical spirit as the rest of the lodge.

Outdoors: terrace, balcony, patio and tropical garden

A covered terrace stretches out in front of the private entrance. The lodge stands on stilts in the tropical garden of the Domaine des Bougainvilliers, Jean-Noël's family estate. From the terrace and balcony, the view runs out to the sea, the garden and the hills.

Good to know before you book

  • Check-in is available around the clock: simply share your arrival time or flight time.
  • Smoke-free home, for a fresh and pleasant interior.
  • Pets welcome on prior request.
  • Shops, a bus stop and village life close by; Grand Baie beach 700 m away; the airport 19 km away.

FAQ

Your questions, our answers

How many guests can stay at the lodge?

Up to 3 guests with the double bed and the sofa bed, or a family of 2 adults, 1 child and 1 baby with the free baby cot on request.

Does the lodge really have a sea view?

Yes: the lodge looks out over the sea, as well as the garden, the mountains and the courtyard. The terrace and balcony are perfect spots to enjoy it.

Can I cook during my stay?

Yes, the private kitchen comes with a hob, fridge, microwave, kettle, toaster and utensils.

Where exactly is the lodge?

At the Domaine des Bougainvilliers in Jeantac, near Grand Baie and Port Mathurin on Rodrigues Island. Grand Baie beach is 700 m away and the airport 19 km.

How do I book direct?

Message Jean-Noël on WhatsApp at +230 5807 1518 with your dates and number of guests: he replies personally with a quote. The lodge can also be booked on Booking.
